Responding to The Call.

Emerging from the late-night silence, Pukaar is a sonic transmission of longing and distance. In the quiet hours when memory drifts like smoke, Aditya Billboard and Sarkar translate the ache of waiting into an intimate, visceral hip-hop anthem.

The raw production by Aircraft Production captures the friction of unspoken words and the vulnerability of being summoned back.
